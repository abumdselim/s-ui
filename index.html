Questions? Answered

Is Linktree the original link in bio tool?

Yes — Linktree pioneered the link in bio category in 2016 and remains the most trusted platform of its kind, used by more than 70M creators, brands and businesses.

Can you get paid and sell things from a Linktree?

Absolutely. You can sell products, collect tips and payments, and earn commission through commerce links — all directly from your Linktree.

Is Linktree safe to use on all of my social media profiles?

Yes. Linktree is trusted and officially supported across Instagram, TikTok, Twitter and more, and every link is automatically scanned for safety.

What makes Linktree better than the other link in bio options?

Powerful analytics, deep customization, integrations with your favorite platforms, and a platform trusted by 70M+ people — all in one simple tool.

How can I drive more traffic to and through my Linktree?

Add your URL to every social bio, share your QR code offline, use auto-reply features, and keep your content fresh to keep audiences clicking.

How many links should I have on my Linktree?

There’s no limit, but 3–5 focused links usually convert best. Lead with what matters most to your audience right now.

Do I need a website to use Linktree?

No website needed. Your Linktree is your home on the internet — set it up in minutes with no code or hosting required.

Where can I download the app?

Linktree is available on the App Store and Google Play so you can manage your link in bio from anywhere.
